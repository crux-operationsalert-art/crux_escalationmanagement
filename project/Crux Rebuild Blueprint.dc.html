Corrective architecture
One outbox table with a unique constraint on the idempotency key, a single sender worker with a daily budget, exponential back-off with a terminal ABANDONED state, and quota failures classified as retry tomorrow — never retry now. A chase e-mail is scheduled by the case's next-due timestamp, not swept for every tick.
Volume correction
You expected thousands of branch e-mails a month, so you chose to spread the send over several days. In fact 693 branches hold a complete five-level matrix, and 722 branches are active at all. At the agreed completeness gate the monthly dispatch is ≈693 e-mails — comfortably inside one day's quota. Recommendation: send in one window, keep the multi-day spread as an automatic fallback only when the eligible count exceeds 1,200.

O · Proposed data architecture

26 tabs become 19 tables — and 1,729 coverage rows become about 40

Three rules govern this model. Every reference is an id, never a name or an e-mail. Nothing derivable is stored. Every row carries where it came from, so a migrated record can always be traced back to its original sheet row.

Recommended stack, given free-tier-only and an existing Google Cloud project: Postgres (Supabase free tier) + a small server-rendered app + one scheduled worker. Postgres gives you the unique constraints that make the duplication structurally impossible, which is the whole point.
